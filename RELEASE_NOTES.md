# LOODY Release Notes

## v1.0.0-stage1 — Public Stage One Release
- **Identity & Theme**: Introduced the Obsidian Black & Emerald design system and Concept A 3D Capital L logo across all 6 brand variants and PWA assets.
- **First-Launch Experience**: Added lightweight particle & 3D L splash sequence with gesture-safe harmonic chime, reduced-motion mode, and skip/replay controls.
- **Model Hub & Routing**: Launched 5 branded models (`Sonic 3`, `Sonic 3.5`, `Lodie 5.5`, `Fables 5.3`, `LOODY Vision 1`) over modular provider adapters.
- **Workspace Core**: Delivered streaming chat, markdown/code rendering, vision attachments, projects, permission-checked MCP registry, built-in tools, honest integration statuses, and English/Arabic RTL support.
- **Security & Quotas**: Added `scrypt` authentication, HTTP-only signed sessions, server-side developer allowlist RBAC, audit logging, and real token accounting with configurable limits up to 1,000,000/day.
- **Stage Two Foundation**: Seeded disabled feature flags for `LOODY Code`, `LOODY Work`, `Skills`, and `Files`.
